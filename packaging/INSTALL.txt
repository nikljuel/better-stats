Better Stats - Installation

1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Copy "BetterStats.app" and the "betterstats" folder
   into the existing "applications" folder on your reader.
3. Eject the reader and open BetterStats once.

On first launch the launcher icon and automatic tracking
are set up. Reboot the reader to activate both.
If automatic tracking interferes with G-sensor rotation,
turn Autostart off in Settings and open Better Stats once
after each reboot to start tracking manually. Restart the
reader once after turning Autostart off.
Future updates install from Settings > Check now.
